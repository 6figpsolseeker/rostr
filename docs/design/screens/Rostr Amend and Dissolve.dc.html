Rules were frozen when this league was created, and an amendment is the only thing that moves one. It takes a signature from every one of the twelve managers. Not a majority, not a quorum — all twelve, and any single refusal ends it.

What an amendment cannot do
— Touch anything that already happened. Results, rosters, standings, waiver order and trade history are league state, not rules, and no signature count rewrites them.
— Apply retroactively. If this passes on Friday, trades already expired under the old deadline stay expired.
— Be pushed through by you. You are the commissioner and it makes no difference here — your signature is one of twelve.
Before you send this

Twelve signatures is a high bar on purpose. Anyone who benefits from the rule as it stands can end this by doing nothing at all, and there is no deadline forcing them to answer.

Most amendments fail. That is the design working, not a bug in it — the alternative is rules that move when someone is losing.

Who has to sign
12 of 12 managers, including you

Every member holding a stake — and a bot holds none. It has no wallet and paid no buy-in, so a bot seat neither signs nor blocks, exactly as it is left out of the veto denominator. Twelve here because all twelve seats are human.

Signatures are visible to the league as they arrive, and so is a refusal. There is no private vote here.

End this league now, with ten weeks played.

Dissolving takes all twelve signatures, the same as an amendment. Four have signed. If it completes, the season stops where it is: no playoffs, no champion, and nothing recorded against anyone's name for the 2026 season.

Exactly what happens
The season Stops at Week 10. Weeks 11–17 are never played, and there is no champion, runner-up or consolation winner.
Your roster The fourteen NFTs stay in your wallet and their transfer restriction relaxes, the same as it would at settlement. They stop being a lineup and become just tokens.
The record Ten weeks of results stay on chain and stay readable. Dissolving ends a league; it does not erase what happened in it.
The pot This league has none. In a pot league every member gets their full stake back and no fee is taken, because a refund is not a settlement.
Afterwards Nothing. A dissolved league cannot be restarted, unfrozen or copied forward. Next season is a new league, created from scratch and frozen again.

Why unanimity, when four people want out

The four who signed are the bottom four. A majority rule would let a losing half end a season a winning half is enjoying, and in a pot league it would let them claw back a stake they were about to lose. So the team in first can refuse, and one refusal is enough — the same property that stops eleven people ending a league to spite the twelfth.
This cannot be undone

A dissolved league is final on chain. There is nobody to appeal to, including us. If you are unsure, not signing costs nothing — the proposal simply sits there.
