2 · Roster

QB 1, RB 2, WR 2, TE 1, FLEX 1, K 1, DEF 1 — nine starters. Bench 5, IR 2. Each slot locks individually at the kickoff of that player's game.

3 · League

Maximum 12 teams, minimum 2 humans. Private, invite only. At most one bot, only to square an odd field, never in a league with a pot.

4 · Draft

Snake, order reversing each round. Fast pace, 90-second pick clock. The order is drawn once from the first Solana block at or after 8:00 PM ET on 22 August — not by the commissioner, and not by rostr. The field locks at the draw. Auto-pick takes the highest available player on your queue, then the best available at your most-needed position.

5 · Season

Weeks 1–14 regular season, 15 quarterfinals, 16 semifinals, 17 championship. Week 18 unused. Head-to-head, higher PPR total wins. The schedule is a seeded round robin generated from this rules hash, so you can recompute the fixtures yourself. Top 6 qualify, seeds 1–2 bye, every round reseeds. Third place is played. The consolation bracket is played.

6 · Tiebreakers

Win percentage, then points for, then head-to-head where the tied teams played an equal number of times, then points against, then lowest team ID. No coin flips.

7 · Transactions

Players return to waivers Tuesday 00:00 ET; blind claims resolve by priority Wednesday 03:00 ET; free agency between. Rolling priority starting in reverse draft order — a failed claim costs nothing. Trades pass through a 48-hour veto window unless one third of uninvolved managers vote against. Trade deadline: end of Week 11. The commissioner has no veto override.

8 · The pot

None. This league plays for nothing. Its rules are still hashed on-chain and members still sign that hash to join — only the escrow is skipped. No money is at stake and none will be.

9 · Autofill

A slot you leave empty is filled at lock, ranked by that week's projection scored under these rules. There is no abandonment rule, no strikes, and no forfeiture. You may turn autofill off for your own team at any time.

10 · The commissioner

Before Week 1 only: name the league, reschedule the draft within its window, add and remove bots, invite and remove members. Never: edit rules, force or reverse trades, override vetoes, set your lineup, adjust waiver order, or move funds.

11 · Contingencies

A cancelled NFL game scores 0 and the matchup stands. Oracle disagreement freezes the week. Stat revisions are absorbed by the finalisation window and do not reopen a finalised week. A championship tie goes to the higher seed. A league that never reaches two humans auto-dissolves. A disputed result goes to a league vote — no commissioner discretion exists.

End of the rule set. Verify against the source

Amending any of this after you join requires the unanimous signed consent of every member holding a stake. Dissolving it requires the same. There is no other route — not a vote the commissioner wins, not an appeal to rostr.
