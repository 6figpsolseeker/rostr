Halvorsen clears a day later than the rest — he landed on waivers Tuesday evening, and a player only clears at the first run a full day after he arrives. Otherwise the league would get no real chance to claim him.

A backup who breaks out on Sunday goes to whoever holds priority on Wednesday morning — not to whoever happens to be refreshing on Sunday night. That is the whole point of the system.

A player you have held for a day or more goes to waivers when you drop him. One held less than that goes straight to free agency — which stops anyone adding a player, cutting him hours later, and re-adding him to sidestep the queue. Nobody can be added or dropped once his own game has kicked off.
