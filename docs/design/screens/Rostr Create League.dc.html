02 Draft Snake, and the order is not yours to draw
Late August — drafts happen before the 10 September kickoff.
Fast runs in one sitting. Slow runs over days.
90 seconds is the floor. The clock is hard — expiry always results in a pick, so a draft never stalls.

The draft order is drawn from the first Solana block at or after your scheduled time — not at creation, and not by you. That block does not exist while teams are still joining, so nobody can know the order in advance. It is drawn once, the field locks at the draw, and anyone can recompute it from the slot and blockhash.

03 Field Up to 12 teams, minimum 2 humans
Available because this league has no pot and the field is currently odd. At most one, and only when the number of managers is odd — adding one to an even league would create the bye it exists to prevent. Removable right up until the draft order is drawn. Turning the pot on removes this option: a bot has no wallet, so a bot in a paying position would leave a share with no recipient. Creation refuses the combination outright.

2 · Roster

QB 1, RB 2, WR 2, TE 1, FLEX 1, K 1, DEF 1 — nine starters. Bench 5, IR 2. Each slot locks individually at the kickoff of that player's game.

3 · League

Maximum 12 teams, minimum 2 humans. Private, invite only. No bots. At most one bot is ever permitted, only to square an odd field, and never in a league with a pot.

4 · Draft

Snake, order reversing each round. Scheduled 22 August 2026, 8:00 PM ET. Fast pace, 90-second pick clock. The order is drawn once from the first Solana block at or after the scheduled time; the field locks at the draw and no team may join afterwards. Auto-pick takes the highest available player on your queue, then the best available at your most-needed position.

5 · Season

Weeks 1–14 regular season, 15 quarterfinals, 16 semifinals, 17 championship. Week 18 unused. Head-to-head, higher PPR total wins, equal totals tie. The schedule is a seeded round robin generated from the rules hash, so anyone holding it can recompute the fixtures. Top 6 of 12 qualify; seeds 1–2 bye. Every round reseeds. Third place is played, not awarded. Consolation bracket played across Weeks 15–17.

6 · Tiebreakers

Win percentage, then points for, then head-to-head where the tied teams played an equal number of times, then points against, then lowest team ID. Deterministic on purpose — no coin flips, because the contract must settle without randomness.

7 · Transactions

Unrostered players return to waivers Tuesday 00:00 ET; blind claims resolve by priority Wednesday 03:00 ET; free agency between. Rolling priority, starting in reverse draft order — a failed claim costs nothing. One-day waiver period. Trades move through escrow with a 48-hour veto window and pass unless one third of uninvolved managers vote against. Trade deadline: end of Week 11. The commissioner has no veto override.

8 · The pot

None. This league plays for nothing. Its rules are still hashed on-chain and members still sign that hash to join — only the escrow is skipped.

9 · Autofill

A slot left empty is filled at lock, ranked by this week's projection scored under these rules, ties broken by ascending player ID, scarce slots filled first. There is no abandonment rule, no strikes, and no forfeiture. Any manager may turn autofill off for their own team.

10 · Commissioner

Before Week 1 only: name the league, schedule the draft within the creation-time window, add and remove bots, invite and remove members. Never: edit rules, force or reverse trades, override vetoes, set another team's lineup, adjust waiver order, or move funds.

11 · Contingencies

A cancelled NFL game scores 0 for affected players and the matchup stands. Oracle disagreement freezes the week pending review. Stat revisions are absorbed by the finalisation window — 48 hours for non-paying weeks, seven days for Weeks 14 and 17 — and do not reopen a finalised week. A championship tie goes to the higher seed. A league that never reaches two humans by the draft date auto-dissolves with full refunds. A disputed result goes to a league vote; no commissioner discretion exists.
