Expiry always results in a pick — that is a frozen rule, and it is the same rule that stops one absent manager stalling eleven others. So the useful thing to show is not an error. It is exactly what will be drafted on your behalf, and what to do in the next thirty seconds.

Why there is no pause button

Pausing on disconnect would be trivially exploitable: closing a laptop would buy unlimited thinking time, and a commissioner could not be trusted to adjudicate whose outage was real. The clock is the same length for everyone, always running, which is why the queue exists.
